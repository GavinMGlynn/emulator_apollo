February 1985, the middle of three. **Eleven chapters, three appendices and an
index**, against Rev 1's eight chapters and two appendices and Rev 4's own
arrangement — so the handbook grew in both directions and no one revision
contains the others.

Opened for the same reason as Rev 1: it is a *hardware* document, and
`SOFTWARE_SHELF_WALK.md` records why both were sitting unlisted.

## What it adds over Rev 1

- **The active segment table grew.** Rev 1: `AST: ARRAY [1..64] OF ASTE_T`.
  Rev 3: `ARRAY [1..N] OF ASTE_T`, `N = 448 or 1984 (DNx60)`. The ASTE gained a
  pointer to the file map, a system type and flag byte at +14, a current length
  and flags at +18, `.dtm` at +1C, `.extdm`/`.event` at +20, `.type_uid`,
  `.acl_uid`, a file lock key at +34 — and on a DNx60 two more fields, an
  **ASTE-to-MSTE back thread chain** at +38 and the **physical address of the
  page map** at +3C, which is the BST chain `[AEGIS]` §11.2.3 describes for
  forward-mapped systems.
- **A disk volume table entry** (`dvte_t` in `disk.prv.pas`): `.state`
  (0 free, 1 being mounted, 2 assigned, 3 mounted), `.dcte` pointer, `.unit`,
  **`.dtype`**, `.blocks_per_vol`, `.blocks_per_track`, `.tracks_per_cylinder`,
  `.current_cyl`, `.owner_proc`, `.lv_base`, the volume's UID, and flags
  `U use_caller_blkhdr`, `N no_crc_retry`, `W write_protect`.
- **A whole peripheral I/O chapter** Rev 1 does not have: device addresses,
  disk parameters, the I/O map and its allocation, both keyboards (880 and
  Low-Profile) with physical *and* translated charts, the magtape controller,
  and a **system configuration pointer at `xxxFF6`** with its configuration
  block, channel control block and parameter block.
- **Per-model chapters** for DN400/420/600 and DN460/660/DSP160 carrying fault
  frames, fault types, fault vectors, floating-point formats, BLT registers,
  display control and status registers, MCSR halves, the cache, CPU control
  registers, the **control panel (CPIO) registers** and an **LED register**.
- Chapter 2's file-system structures are more numerous than Rev 1's: **VTOC
  block, VTOC entry, VTOC header, VTOC map entry and VTOC index**, where Rev 1
  has the entry alone.

## What it does not add, checked rather than assumed

**Its disk parameter table is superseded.** Chapter 6 tabulates every approved
drive by Dtype class — 000/001/006/007 PRIAM on the 14" ring/disk interface,
100/103/104/105 on 8" ANSI, 200/201/202 NEC and Micropolis on 8" SMD, 300-306
Micropolis/Fujitsu/Maxtor/Vertex on 5¼" ST412, plus floppy `CTYPE=1` and the
Intel and Xylogics SMD controllers at `CTYPE=4` — with cylinders, heads,
sectors, total blocks, and then **track/average/maximum seek, rpm, latency,
transfer rate and average read**, where "average read = Avg. Seek + Avg. latency
+ Sector Time".

**Every drive in it predates the two this core models.** `ap_omti.h` already
derives its timings from `008778-03` Table 6-5 (the **Maxtor EXT-4380**, 348
Mbyte) and Table 6-4 (the **Micropolis 1355**, 155 Mbyte), and already records
that `002398-04` p. 6-3 — Rev 4's version of this same table — gives
`604 Max 380: T-to-T 4, AVG 27, MAX 52, RPM 3600, AVG LATENCY 8.33, TRANSFER
RATE 1.25, AVG READ 35.3`, agreeing with Table 6-5 except on the average seek,
where the two Apollo documents are 10% apart and the code says so. **Rev 3's
table stops at the 190 Mbyte Maxtor and has no row for either drive.** So it
confirms the table's *shape* across three revisions and supplies no figure this
core can use. Recorded as a negative result, which is what most of a third
revision should be.

## One question raised and not answered

Rev 1's physical volume label matches `media/dn5500-invol-done.awd` byte for
byte at +00 (version `0001`), +02/+04 (`"APOLLO"`) and +08 (the 32-byte name,
`DN5500`) — see `002398-01_WALK.md`. **The later fields do not.** The image
reads `00000504` where Rev 1 puts `.dtype` at +30, `00013F4C` (81,740) where it
puts `.blocks_per_pvol` at +34, and `0012 000F` (18, 15) at +38 for blocks per
track and tracks per cylinder — but the volume INVOL built is 326,956 kB, which
is not 81,740 blocks of any size this project uses.

Either the label's tail moved between 1983 and SR10.4, or `+34` is not what Rev
1's figure says it is. **Not resolved**, and Rev 3 does not carry a physical
volume label page to compare — its file-system chapter has the logical label and
the VTOC structures, and the physical label appears only in Rev 1 among the two
revisions opened. `002398-04` is walked whole and its record should be checked
for a third copy before this is pursued further.
