## Resume here

**Next page: PDF 6.** Then 7–10, then the front matter at 1–3.

**Two findings to act on when the walk finishes** (not before — the rest of the
document may bear on both):

1. **`AP_ATMAP_ENTRIES` is 1024 and should be 2048 for the DS5500.** Table 2-5
   gives the map 4 KB. `ap_atmap.h` already records the disagreement and names
   the cost: the entry count is what the state hash walks, so widening it
   **re-baselines the identity hash**. Check whether the firmware evidence at
   `01002BF6` — `MOVE.W A0,(A0)+` from `017000` to `0177FE` — is a DS3500 probe
   rather than a DS5500 one before changing anything; the two machines may
   genuinely differ and the constant may need to become per-model.
2. **DESKTOP VISUALIZATION SPACE is unmodelled.** Decide whether it is a region
   to place (and report unmapped rather than zero, as this core does elsewhere)
   or a named gap. 96 MB reported as ordinary unmapped space is not wrong, but
   it is unnamed, and a trace cannot say what the firmware reached for.

**Also noted, outside this document's scope but raised by it**: the DS5500 has a
**SCSI bus** and this core models none. That is a subsystem-sized absence and
should be a plan item in its own right if the DS5500 is to be a supported model.
