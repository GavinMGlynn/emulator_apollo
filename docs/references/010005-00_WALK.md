## Method, and the one trap this document has already sprung

- **Read the page images.** The scan is 300 ppi and renders legibly at native.
  Do not render below it — the `002398-04` keyboard charts cost three sessions
  to that mistake.
- **Text extraction is for *searching*, never for reading.** This document
  destroys superscripts: §2.1 step 7's `2^14` extracts as `214`, a
  plausible-looking number wrong by three orders of magnitude. It was caught
  only by checking the arithmetic against the stated 10.9 msec. Assume every
  exponent in this document is mangled in the text layer.
- **Check figures against the ring's own clock.** 12 Mbit/s gives a byte time of
  14,361,600 time-base units exactly; any duration the document states should
  reproduce from a power of two times that. That is what confirmed finding 139
  and it is a free check on every other figure here.
