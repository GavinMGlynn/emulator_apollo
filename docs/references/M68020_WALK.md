### Method note: proving a page blank without reading it

Two pages so far are section-end fillers. A blank page still has to be *opened* —
"a range cannot be characterised without opening it" is this project's most
expensive repeated lesson — but it does not have to be read as an image. Render
at 100 dpi and count dark pixels:

    pdftoppm -r 100 -png -f N -l N <pdf> out
    # p. 55: dark pixels: 1 of 631400

One speck in 631,400 is a blank page, established rather than assumed, for a
fraction of the cost of looking at it. p. 31 was read as an image before this was
worked out; p. 55 by the count.

**And it is a check, not an optimisation — it paid for itself at pp. 448 and
450.** Both return *nothing* from `pdftotext`, and both sit past §11 and every
appendix where a section table would predict filler. The count read 45,150 and
40,215 dark pixels, and they are §10's **Figure 10-5 and Figure 10-6**, the read
and write cycle timing diagrams, bound as foldouts **220 pages from the section
they belong to**. A walk that read §10's stated range and trusted the text layer
would have missed both and never known. So the rule is: **count the pixels of
every page you are about to call blank**, because the pages a text layer calls
empty are exactly the image-only ones.

Started because the deferral that kept it closed had a false premise. The plan
item read "Phase 2b and Phase 7 parts ... **Deferred until those processors are
built**" — and the 68020 *is* built into the model table already:
`src/core/model/ap_model.c` gives **three** rows `.cpu = AP_CPU_M68020` (DN3000,
DN4000/DS4000 and a third), and `ap_model.h` cites "MC68020 User's Manual §7.1.1
and §1" for cache sizes that are live in that table today. A part with rows in
the model table is not a future part.

## The citation audit, which is where this walk starts

The rule the batch item sets — "do the citation audit first, per document; it is
one grep" — and its answer here is the opposite of `[030]`'s. There, §7-§9 and
§11 held 88 of 90 citations and were *verification*, which halved the job.

Here: `src/` and `tests/` mention the 68020 **473 times**, and only **six** of
those cite this manual with a place — §1, §7.1.1, §9.2.1 and Appendix D. The
other 467 are prose about the part, written from `[030]`'s and `[PRM]`'s
descriptions of what the 68020 does differently. **So there is almost no
verification to lean on and the 452 pages are new reading.** Recorded because it
is the estimate the walk is planned against, not an aside.
